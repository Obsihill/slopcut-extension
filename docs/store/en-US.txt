Extension name
SlopCut

Short description — matches the en manifest message
Hide products from sellers you choose on supported marketplaces. Block beside seller names and manage your list locally.


Detailed description
Keep marketplace listings focused on sellers you want to see.

SlopCut adds a small blocking button beside seller names on Unity Asset Store, BOOTH, CGTrader, Fab and TurboSquid. Clicking it hides matching product cards in your browser. It does not modify your marketplace account, submit reports, or block a seller through the marketplace's own account system.

Features
• Block sellers from listing cards and supported product detail pages.
• Watch newly blocked cards dissolve into dark dust while remaining cards move into place without reloading the listing.
• Pause hiding globally or for each supported site.
• Search and filter your blocked list, add sellers by URL or ID, and unblock them.
• Export a local JSON backup and merge a backup into your current list.
• Use a classic Windows XP-style settings page with system, light, and dark appearance.

Your list and settings stay in the current browser profile. The extension has no developer-operated server, analytics, advertising trackers, or sign-up requirement. Exported backup files are controlled by you.

The name is displayed as 딸깍컷 in Korean and SlopCut otherwise. The management interface, button labels, and status messages currently use Korean; the blocking button is labeled 차단.

TurboSquid seller labels are read from public quick previews for cards near the viewport. If the website blocks those requests, a label may be delayed or unavailable.

Please note: SlopCut does not detect or classify AI-generated content. Only sellers you select are hidden. Marketplace result counts and page numbers are unchanged. Compatibility can vary with page redesigns and custom BOOTH shop themes. Blocking the main seller on a detail page returns to the previous page after the effect, or the marketplace home if no previous page exists. Previously blocked products are hidden immediately. Explicitly requested effects also play when OS reduced motion is enabled.

SlopCut is not affiliated with these marketplaces or Microsoft.
